# RejectionMaxxing 🌟  
**Empowering Young Industry Entrants Through Positivity and Community**  

---

## 📌 What is RejectionMaxxing?  
RejectionMaxxing is a platform designed to help young professionals and industry newcomers **turn rejection into motivation**. By rewarding users for sharing their rejection stories, celebrating progress, and fostering a supportive community, we aim to **redefine resilience** in the face of setbacks.  

---

## 🎯 Core Features  
- **Authentication**  
  - Secure sign-up/login for users to track their journey.  
- **Multiuser Ranking System**  
  - Leaderboards to celebrate users who embrace rejection and grow from it.  
- **Payment System**  
  - Purchase virtual "brag stickers" or exclusive content to celebrate milestones.  
- **Screenshot Uploads**  
  - Share screenshots of rejections (e.g., job denials, project failures) or successes to earn points.  
- **Points & Rewards**  
  - Earn points for:  
    - Uploading screenshots (weekly/daily events).  
    - Participating in community challenges.  
    - Networking with other users (e.g., peer reviews, mentorship).  
- **Speech/Text UI**  
  - Interactive prompts and motivational messages to guide users through their journey.  

---

## 🧰 Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)  
- **Responsive Design**  
  - Fully functional on mobile, tablet, and desktop.  
- **Backend Infrastructure**  
  - Secure storage for user data, screenshots, and transaction history.  
  - Backend to manage client secrets (e.g., API keys, payment gateways).  
- **Cybersecurity**  
  - **PII Protection**: Encryption for sensitive user data (e.g., names, emails).  
  - **Content Moderation**: AI/ML tools to flag inappropriate images/text in uploads.  

---

## 💰 Revenue Model  
- **Context-Specific Ads**  
  - Targeted ads for career resources, mental health services, or industry-related tools.  
- **Discounts & Premium Features**  
  - Offer discounts on courses, tools, or exclusive stickers for active users.  

---

## 🛡️ Cybersecurity Priorities  
- **Data Privacy**  
  - Compliant with GDPR/CCPA standards.  
  - Secure authentication (OAuth 2.0, 2FA).  
- **Content Safety**  
  - Automated image/text analysis to block harmful or inappropriate content.  
  - User reporting system for community moderation.
